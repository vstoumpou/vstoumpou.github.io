Vasiliki Stoumpou

PhD candidate · Operations Research Center

I work at the intersection of machine learning and optimization. My research is guided by three principles: learning systems should make use of all the information available, be understandable to the people using them, and connect prediction to decision-making. I am particularly interested in these questions in healthcare.

Teaching and course development

I have taught optimization, machine learning, and analytics to undergraduate, graduate, and Executive MBA students. My guiding principle is that understanding comes before complexity. I aim to make technical ideas intuitive enough that students can reason through them independently and apply them to new problems.
